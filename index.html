🌿 Before You Start — Discover the Garden

Welcome to the KPMG Wellness Garden! Located at East Coast Park Area D, this inclusive garden was developed in partnership with NParks for people of all generations to enjoy the natural coastal environment while supporting health and well-being.

Look around as you hunt: the garden's design includes reused rocks and upcycled logs, while native plants such as Sea Lettuce and Silver Bush support coastal habitat restoration.

🌿 How to play

Goal: Complete all four challenges.

Found it: ticks the challenge off, turns its card green and adds the FOUND! stamp.

Take photo: opens the phone camera where supported. The photo is added to the card and automatically marks the challenge as found.

Retake: take another photo. Remove: delete a saved shot. Multiple photos are supported for the insect and squirrel challenges.

Photo wall: this standalone version creates a wall from photos on the current device. To make every team's photos appear automatically for everyone, publish the page with a shared backend/database.
